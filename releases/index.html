Version 1.0.1 (2)

October 2026 · Fix

  • Barcode scanning works again. The camera now reads ISBN barcodes in the version installed from Google Play.
  • Small fixes and updates under the hood.

Version 1.0.0 (1)

October 2026 · First release

  • Scan the ISBN barcode on a book, or type an ISBN, to add it to your Grimoire with its own Dex number
  • Details and covers from Google Books and Open Library; books with no ISBN can be added by hand
  • Owned, Wishlist and Borrowed tabs, with search, sort and filters
  • Read status as moon phases, half-star ratings, tags and vendors
  • Special editions with the same ISBN kept as separate books
  • Your own cover photos, stored only in the app
  • Works offline: books scanned without a connection fill in later
  • Export to CSV, JSON or a ZIP backup with photos, and import from CSV
  • Day, Night or System theme under a living night sky
