Know thy shelves.

A grimoire for every book you own

Scan the ISBN barcode on the back of a book and SpineDex fills in the title, author and cover. Keep track of what you own, wish for and borrow, how far you’ve read, and how much you loved it. Special editions with the same ISBN each get their own place, so you always know exactly which copies are on your shelves.

  • Scan barcodes or type an ISBN; add books with no ISBN by hand
  • Read status as moon phases, half-star ratings, tags and vendors
  • Your own cover photos, stored only in the app
  • Works offline: scanned books fill in when you’re back online
  • Export to CSV, JSON or a ZIP backup with photos
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Your library stays on your phone.
