SpineDex

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

In short: SpineDex has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your library stays on your phone. The only thing that leaves your phone is the ISBN of a book you scan or type, which is sent to public book databases to look up its details.

This policy explains how the SpineDex app for Android (“SpineDex”, “the app”, “we”) handles information. If you have questions, write to hello.tinytinstudio@gmail.com.

Information stored on your phone

Everything you add to SpineDex is saved only in the app’s private storage on your phone. This includes your books and their details, read status, ratings, tags, vendors, edition labels, cover photos you take or pick, and your settings. We do not run any servers for SpineDex, and we never receive, see or store any of it.

Camera and photos

  • Camera: used to read the ISBN barcode on a book and, if you choose, to take a cover photo. Barcodes are read on your phone. The camera is only on while you are on the scan screen or taking a photo.
  • Photos: when you choose a cover from your gallery, the app reads only the photo you pick.
  • Cover photos are shrunk, saved inside the app and never uploaded. They are not added to your gallery.

Looking up books online

When you scan or type an ISBN, the app sends that ISBN to these services to find the book’s title, author, publisher, year, page count and cover:

  • Google Books (Google LLC), covered by the Google Privacy Policy
  • Open Library (Internet Archive), covered by the Internet Archive Terms of Use

Cover images are then downloaded from those services and saved in the app so they show offline. Like any website you visit, these services receive standard technical information such as your IP address. SpineDex sends nothing else with a lookup: no name, account, device identifier or anything from your library. If your phone is offline, the ISBN is kept on your phone and looked up later.

Exporting and importing

When you export your library (CSV, JSON or a ZIP backup with photos), the file is created on your phone and handed to your phone’s share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send or save it. CSV files you import are read on your phone.

Backups by your phone

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include SpineDex’s data in your own device backup, which is stored in your Google account under Google’s terms. We cannot access these backups.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete any book, and its cover photo, in the app. Uninstalling SpineDex removes all of its data from your phone. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

SpineDex is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes on Google Play.
